# Tufts University Brand Guidelines

## Overview

The Tufts brand is more than a logo, color palette, or visual identity. It encompasses every interaction, communication, and experience associated with the university across digital, print, and multimedia channels.

The purpose of these guidelines is to ensure consistency, strengthen recognition, and maintain the integrity of the university's identity across all schools, departments, centers, and administrative units.

---

# Brand Foundation

## Brand Statement

Tufts University is a community of active and engaged scholars and creators who drive innovation, discovery, and collaboration to make meaningful individual and global impact.

## Brand Objectives

All communications should reinforce:

- Academic excellence
- Innovation
- Discovery
- Collaboration
- Interdisciplinary thinking
- Community engagement
- Global impact
- Inclusivity
- Accessibility

---

# Voice and Tone

## Core Voice Attributes

The Tufts voice should be:

- Open
- Clear
- Personal
- Authentic
- Inclusive
- Welcoming
- Collaborative
- Thoughtful

## Communication Principles

Content should:

### Engage Audiences

Use:

- Clear language
- Conversational language
- Accessible language

Avoid:

- Excessive jargon
- Bureaucratic language
- Overly academic language when unnecessary

### Be Concise

Content should:

- Tell the full story
- Avoid unnecessary complexity
- Focus on key messages

### Demonstrate Expertise

Communications should:

- Showcase accomplishments
- Highlight expertise
- Avoid sounding authoritarian
- Avoid institutional self-importance

### Be Audience-Focused

Content should:

- Build relationships
- Invite engagement
- Encourage dialogue
- Put audience needs first

### Tell Authentic Stories

Content should:

- Highlight real people
- Reflect diverse perspectives
- Respectfully represent community members
- Emphasize meaningful impact

# Logos

## Purpose

The Tufts University logo serves as the primary visual identifier for the university.

The logo should appear on:

- Marketing materials
- Publications
- Websites
- Presentations
- Communications
- Merchandise

---

# Official Logo Requirements

## Approved Usage

Only official logo files provided by University Communications and Marketing may be used.

Designers must never:

- Recreate the logo
- Draw the logo manually
- Rebuild the typography
- Scan printed versions
- Modify official artwork

---

# Logo Clear Space

A mandatory clear space must surround the logo.

## Requirement

Minimum clear space:

```text
Height of the capital "T" in the logo
```

No elements may enter this area.

This includes:

- Text
- Graphics
- Borders
- Images
- Decorative elements

---

# Logo Prohibitions

Never:

## Distort

Do not:

- Stretch
- Compress
- Skew
- Rotate

## Redesign

Do not:

- Modify typography
- Replace elements
- Add symbols
- Add punctuation

## Create Composite Logos

Do not:

- Combine logo with artwork
- Merge logo with event graphics
- Attach department graphics
- Add custom illustrations

## Replace Elements

Do not:

- Replace "University"
- Replace letterforms
- Modify spacing

---

# Logo Colors

The official logo should appear only in:

- Tufts Blue
- White
- Black

No other colors are permitted.

---

# University Seal

## Purpose

The university seal is reserved for formal institutional uses.

---

## Approved Uses

The seal may appear on:

- Diplomas
- Legal contracts
- Presidential communications
- Official ceremonial materials
- Approved plaques
- Approved flags
- Approved furniture

---

## Restricted Uses

The seal should NOT appear on:

- Department websites
- Marketing materials
- Student organization branding
- Presentations
- Event graphics
- Social media graphics
- General communications

---

## Seal Rules

No unit may:

- Create logos using the seal
- Incorporate the seal into branding
- Scan seal artwork
- Reproduce the seal independently

Official artwork must come from University Communications and Marketing.

---

# Lockup System

## Purpose

Lockups visually represent relationships between:

- The university
- Schools
- Divisions
- Departments
- Centers

The system reinforces organizational hierarchy.

---

# Parent Brand Strategy

Tufts follows a parent-brand model.

Every unit should clearly appear as part of:

```text
Tufts University
```

No school, center, department, or initiative should create an independent visual identity that competes with the university.

---

# Lockup Structure

## Required Elements

Every lockup contains:

### Element 1

```text
Tufts University Logo
```

### Element 2

```text
School or Administrative Unit Name
```

Separated by:

```text
Vertical Divider
```

---

## Optional Third Element

A third level may be added:

```text
Department
Center
Institute
```

Example:

```text
Tufts University | School of Engineering
                  Department of Computer Science
```

---

# Academic Unit Lockups

Order must always be:

```text
Tufts University
↓
School
↓
Department
```

Example:

```text
Tufts University
School of Engineering
Department of Computer Science
```

---

# Administrative Unit Lockups

Order must always be:

```text
Tufts University
↓
Division
↓
Department
```

---

# Lockup Variations

Approved layouts:

### Horizontal

Preferred version.

### Vertical

Used when space is constrained.

### Stacked

Used when horizontal space is unavailable.

---

# Lockup Restrictions

Never:

- Create custom lockups
- Rearrange hierarchy
- Omit university branding
- Create independent logos
- Modify typography
- Distort layouts

Primary institutional color.
